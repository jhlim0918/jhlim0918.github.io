# Research

## Conditions for Planetesimal Formation by the Streaming Instability in Protoplanetary Disks

My primary research explores the formation of planetesimals within protoplanetary disks. Planetesimals are 1-100 km sized building blocks of planets and thought be formed by the gravitational collapse of small, millimeter-to-centimeter-sized dust grains. While several mechansims have been proposed, the streaming instabiltiy is a leading candidate that can lead to the gravitational collapse by efficiently concentrating dust grains to the point of the collapse. In the series of this work, I investigated the conditions, mainly grain sizes, their abundance, and turbulence strength, under which the streaming instability produces planetesimals in protoplaentary disks. By considering both [laminar](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025arXiv250918270L/abstract) and [turbulent](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024ApJ...969..130L/abstract) disks in 3D simulations, I estabilished the planetesimal formation conditions in a broad range of disk environments.
